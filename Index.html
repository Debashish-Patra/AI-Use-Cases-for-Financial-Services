Plain-English glossary
Lead
Someone who has shown interest but is not yet a customer.
KYC
The identity checks a customer completes to open an account.
CRM
The system where a company keeps customer and lead records.
SIP
A regular monthly investment of a fixed amount.
Compliance
The team that makes sure messages and offers follow the rules.
Comparison group
A small group of customers who do not get a campaign, so you can see what difference it made.
